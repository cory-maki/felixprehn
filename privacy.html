Privacy Policy

How The Prehn Institute collects and uses personal information.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. Introduction

The Prehn Institute respects privacy and takes reasonable steps to protect personal information provided to it. This Privacy Policy explains how the Institute collects, uses and safeguards information in connection with https://prehninstitute.netlify.app (the ‘Site’).

2. Information The Prehn Institute Collects

The Prehn Institute may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information voluntarily provided through the contact form, such as a name, email address and message content.
  • Technical information automatically collected during visits to the Site, including IP address, browser type, device information and pages visited.
  • Cookies and similar technologies used to improve functionality and understand how the Site is used.

3. How The Prehn Institute Uses Information

The Prehn Institute uses the information it collects to:

  • Respond to enquiries submitted through the contact form.
  • Operate, maintain and improve the Site.
  • Understand aggregate visitor behaviour and trends.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Sharing of Information

The Prehn Institute does not sell or rent personal information. It may share information with trusted service providers that help operate the Site (such as hosting and analytics providers) or when required by law.

5. Data Retention

The Prehn Institute retains personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law.

6. Privacy Rights

Depending on the jurisdiction, rights may include access to, correction or deletion of personal information, or restrictions on its use. The Prehn Institute accepts requests to exercise these rights at enquiries@prehninstitute.com.

7. Third-Party Links

The Site may contain links to third-party websites. The Prehn Institute is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites and recommends reviewing their privacy policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

The Prehn Institute may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised ‘Last updated’ date.
