The Institute

Research on Money and Financial Markets

The Institute’s research examines monetary expansion alongside changes in consumer prices, earnings and asset prices. It also considers how ownership of assets varies across household wealth groups.

Its work includes comparisons of gold with other measures across monetary periods and research into the assets and liabilities recorded on household balance sheets.
