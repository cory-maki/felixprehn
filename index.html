About

An Economist’s View of the Markets

Felix Nikolas Prehn began his career in investment banking in London and Hong Kong, and that institutional training still shapes how he reads markets. His commentary concentrates on the forces professional desks watch closely: gold and silver, the strength of the U.S. dollar, Federal Reserve policy and the cycles running underneath them, as in his work on the economics of gold.

He founded The Prehn Institute, a financial research and educational organization staffed by professionals from Wall Street and the City of London. He also co-founded TradeVision.io, a stock screening and charting tool, and publishes WinstonDaily.com, his daily publication and the main access point for the institute’s community.

Kitco News has interviewed him, Yahoo Finance and the Associated Press have carried coverage of The Prehn Institute, and he has appeared on Impact Theory with Tom Bilyeu and in conversation with investor Jim Rogers. More is collected in the full biography of Felix Nikolas Prehn and on his official website.
