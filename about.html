Background

Trained in London and Hong Kong

Felix Nikolas Prehn is an economist and former investment banker whose professional training took place in two of the world’s main financial centres, London and Hong Kong. Institutional banking gave him a practitioner’s view of how capital moves, how risk is priced, and how decisions made by central banks pass through to currencies, bonds and commodities.

That foundation is the starting point for his public market commentary. Rather than reacting to daily price moves, his analysis asks the questions a banking desk would ask: what is driving liquidity, where real interest rates are heading, and how the positioning of large investors has shifted. A summary of his career and current work is available on his LinkedIn profile.

Market Focus

Gold, Silver, the Dollar and the Fed

His commentary returns consistently to a defined set of subjects. Gold and silver sit at the centre, examined as monetary assets whose prices respond to interest rates, currency strength and central bank demand. The U.S. dollar and Federal Reserve policy form the second pillar, since changes in either tend to ripple through every other asset class.

He also studies long-term market cycles, placing current conditions in the context of earlier periods of tightening, easing and inflation. His essay on how he reads the gold market shows how these pieces fit together, and shorter observations appear in his posts on X.

Research & Education

The Prehn Institute

Felix Nikolas Prehn founded The Prehn Institute, a financial research and educational organization. It is staffed by professionals from Wall Street and the City of London, and its work joins two activities: publishing research on the financial markets and providing instruction in how those markets function.

The institute serves people who want market research and education from practitioners with institutional backgrounds. Its veterans programme is one supporting initiative among its activities. A closer look at the institute’s research and education model sets out how the two sides connect.

Media

A Documented Media Record

His work has been covered by several financial outlets. Kitco News has interviewed him on precious metals and the wider economy, while Yahoo Finance and the Associated Press have carried coverage of The Prehn Institute. In August and September 2026 he appeared on Kitco News and on Impact Theory with Tom Bilyeu, and he has held a recorded conversation with investor Jim Rogers on stock markets, inflation and gold.

Alongside these appearances, Felix Nikolas Prehn maintains a steady output of his own. He publishes weekly analysis on his YouTube channel and records the Felix Nikolas Prehn Podcast. Readers looking for an overview of his press coverage will find the outlets and formats set out in detail.

Platforms & Values

Tools, Publications and Animal Rescue

He co-founded TradeVision.io, a stock screening and charting tool that gives members of The Prehn Institute a practical resource for market analysis. He also publishes WinstonDaily.com, his daily publication and the primary access point for the institute’s community.

Outside the markets, he is a public advocate for animal rescue. Winston, the golden retriever he adopted, is at the centre of that personal commitment, and the cause appears from time to time on his Instagram account.
