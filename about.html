About

The Prehn Institute

The Prehn Institute is an independent research institution. It transitioned from Goat Academy to The Prehn Institute in September 2026.

Founder

Felix Nikolas Prehn

The Prehn Institute is an independent research institution founded by Felix Nikolas Prehn. He is a tech founder: he co-founded TradeVision.io (Acetrader Inc), founded Winston Daily and The Prehn Institute, and publishes weekly on YouTube. It publishes research on the financial markets and provides instruction in professional market practice.

Research

Money, Prices and Household Assets

In The Hidden Regressive Tax, Felix Nikolas Prehn examines monetary expansion alongside changes in consumer prices, earnings and asset prices. The paper also considers how gains in household assets are distributed across wealth groups.

His research examines the composition of household holdings, including deposits, corporate shares and real estate. These categories provide different perspectives on the assets held by each wealth group.

The paper uses the Federal Reserve’s Distributional Financial Accounts to examine household assets across wealth groups. It considers those holdings alongside changes in prices over the period covered by the research.
