Felix Nikolas Prehn and The Prehn Institute: Connecting Financial Research and Education

📅 September 29, 2026
✎ Felix Nikolas Prehn
⌚ 5 min read

Felix Nikolas Prehn founded The Prehn Institute to bring two activities under one roof: researching the financial markets and explaining how they work. The Prehn Institute is a financial research and educational organization, and the connection between those two functions defines its approach.

Its founder brings the perspective of an economist and former investment banker trained in London and Hong Kong. The staff share that institutional grounding, with professionals drawn from Wall Street and the City of London. The result is an organisation that looks at markets from the practitioner's side, with the habits and methods of the trading floor and the research desk.

Research Grounded in Institutional Practice

The research side of the institute follows the subjects that run through Felix Nikolas Prehn's own analysis: gold and silver, the U.S. dollar, Federal Reserve policy and long-term market cycles. These are areas where institutional desks have well-developed frameworks, and the institute's work draws on those frameworks to examine current conditions rather than to issue calls on individual securities.

Research of this kind focuses on mechanisms. It asks how an interest rate decision moves through currencies and commodities, or how a change in central bank demand alters the balance of a market. His framework for gold is one example of the method in practice, built around real yields, the dollar and official-sector buying.

Education That Explains How Markets Work

The educational side of The Prehn Institute provides instruction in how financial markets function. The emphasis is on understanding: the structure of markets, the role of central banks and the relationships between asset classes, so that research findings can be read in context rather than taken on trust.

The two halves depend on each other. Research without explanation is difficult to use, and explanation without current research quickly goes out of date. Practitioners from Wall Street and the City of London are well placed to supply both, which is why the institute is staffed the way it is. The model reflects his own institutional banking background, where analysis is expected to be rigorous and usable at the same time.

Tools and Publications Around the Institute

Two platforms extend the institute's work. TradeVision.io, the stock screening and charting tool Felix Nikolas Prehn co-founded, gives members a practical resource for applying market analysis. WinstonDaily.com, his daily publication, serves as the primary access point for The Prehn Institute community and carries its regular market commentary.

The institute also runs a veterans programme as one of its supporting initiatives. Public coverage of the organisation, including items carried by Yahoo Finance and the Associated Press, is set out in the founder’s media record, and further background is available on his personal website.

Conclusion

The Prehn Institute was built on a simple premise: market research and market education are more useful together than apart. Founded by Felix Nikolas Prehn and staffed by professionals from Wall Street and the City of London, it applies institutional methods to both.
